disposed by t.TempDir() cleanup; no binary fixture file is checked in.

Register the adapter via cmd/tm/cli/registry.go AllAdapters["goose"]:
  $GOOSE_PATH_ROOT/data/sessions/sessions.db
or one of the home-directory defaults (see goose.go).
